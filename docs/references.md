# Academic References & Bibliography

1. **Vincent, Luc. (1993).**  
   *Morphological grayscale reconstruction in image analysis: Applications and efficient algorithms.*  
   IEEE Transactions on Image Processing, 2(2), 176–201.  
   DOI: [10.1109/83.217222](https://doi.org/10.1109/83.217222)  
   *Foundational paper introducing the FIFO priority-queue algorithm for fast morphological reconstruction.*

2. **Soille, Pierre. (2003).**  
   *Morphological Image Analysis: Principles and Applications (2nd ed.).*  
   Springer-Verlag, Berlin, Heidelberg.  
   ISBN: 978-3-540-42988-3.  
   *Comprehensive textbook detailing geodesic dilation, geodesic erosion, and top-hat by reconstruction.*

3. **Serra, Jean. (1982).**  
   *Image Analysis and Mathematical Morphology.*  
   Academic Press, London.  
   ISBN: 978-0126372403.  
   *The classical text establishing mathematical morphology on complete lattices.*

4. **Gonzalez, Rafael C., & Woods, Richard E. (2018).**  
   *Digital Image Processing (4th Edition).*  
   Pearson Education.  
   ISBN: 978-0133356724.  
   *Covers CLAHE, Otsu thresholding, connected component labeling, and morphological feature extraction.*

5. **Matheron, Georges. (1975).**  
   *Random Sets and Integral Geometry.*  
   John Wiley & Sons, New York.  
   *Theoretical origin of mathematical morphology and granulometry.*

6. **Zucker, Steven W. (1976).**  
   *Region growing: Childhood and adolescence.*  
   Computer Graphics and Image Processing, 5(3), 382–399.  
   *Review of region segmentation paradigms.*

7. **Dice, Lee R. (1945).**  
   *Measures of the amount of ecologic association between species.*  
   Ecology, 26(3), 297–302.  
   *Definition of the Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC).*

8. **Jaccard, Paul. (1912).**  
   *The distribution of the flora in the alpine zone.*  
   New Phytologist, 11(2), 37–50.  
   *Definition of the Intersection over Union (IoU) metric.*
